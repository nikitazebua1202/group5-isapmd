❄️ Winter Collection

Welcome the season of quiet wonder with our radiant winter blooms. Each flower reflects resilience, hope, and the fresh beginnings that arise even in the heart of winter.

Top Winter Arrangements

  1. Snowlit Evergreen Whisper

    A cool-toned bouquet with white roses, hellebores (often called the Christmas rose), and silvery-blue eryngium to give an icy, frosted look. Soft evergreen foliage such as pine or fir and a touch of white berries complete the wintry, woodland feel.

  2. Crimson Hearth Glow

    A rich, warm mix of deep red amaryllis, classic red roses, and burgundy chrysanthemums for a cozy “by the fireplace” vibe. Dark greenery and red hypericum berries add texture and warmth, making it perfect for December or holiday gifting.

  3. Frosted Moonlight Roses

    A mostly white bouquet of roses, lisianthus, and ranunculus, highlighting soft, layered petals that feel romantic against a snowy backdrop. Accents of dusty miller or eucalyptus bring in a frosty silver tone that suits evening winter events.

  4. Nordic Berry Woodland

    This bouquet mixes creamy roses with hypericum berries and seasonal greenery like holly or pine, giving a rustic, forest-inspired look. The contrast of pale blooms with red berries and deep green foliage feels both festive and natural.

Winter Care Essentials

  • Water plants only when the top soil feels dry to avoid overwatering in winter.
  • Place flowers where they receive the most natural light, such as near bright windows.
  • Boost humidity around plants with a humidifier, pebble tray, or by grouping them together.
  • Shield outdoor flowers from frost with mulch, covers, or protective cloths.
  • Fertilize lightly and less often, since growth naturally slows in colder months.
  • Regularly remove dead leaves and spent blooms to keep plants healthy and prevent disease.

🌸 Autumn Collection

We welcomed the passing autumn with our warm, earthy blooms. Each flower captured the quiet beauty of the season gone by and the strength found in embracing change.

Autumn Arrangements

  1. Chrysanthemum Glory Bouquet

    A stunning display of burgundy, orange, and golden chrysanthemums arranged in a rustic autumn style. Perfect for Thanksgiving celebrations and fall gatherings.

  2. Harvest Moon Collection

    Warm-toned marigolds mixed with deep red dahlias create a cozy, harvest-inspired arrangement that captures the essence of autumn evenings.

  3. Autumn Sunset Bouquet

    Rich copper and amber zinnias paired with burnt orange asters for a breathtaking gradient effect reminiscent of fall sunsets.

  4. Rustic Fall Garden

    A charming combination of mixed autumn blooms with wheat stalks, dried leaves, and seasonal berries for a natural, countryside feel.

Autumn Care Essentials

  • Keep flowers away from cold drafts and heating vents
  • Change water every 2-3 days to prevent bacteria buildup
  • Trim stems regularly for better water absorption
  • Remove wilted blooms and fallen leaves promptly
  • Use lukewarm water instead of cold water
